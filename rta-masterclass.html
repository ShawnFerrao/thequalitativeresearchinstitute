Overview

Move beyond understanding thematic analysis to confidently putting it into practice

Reflexive Thematic Analysis (RTA) is a flexible and widely used approach to analysing qualitative data. However, understanding its theoretical foundations and knowing how to apply them in practice can be challenging.

In this comprehensive two-day masterclass, Dr Claire Moran guides you through the principles and processes of Reflexive Thematic Analysis, combining theoretical understanding with practical, hands-on learning.

Through guided activities, interactive discussions and real examples from Claire's own research, you'll explore how to engage meaningfully with qualitative data, develop analytical interpretations and construct themes. You'll also strengthen your understanding of the methodological decisions that underpin rigorous, reflexive qualitative analysis.

Who it's for

Who is this masterclass for?

This masterclass is particularly suited to researchers who are preparing to analyse, or are currently analysing, qualitative data. It is designed for:

  • PhD and postgraduate researchers undertaking qualitative research.
  • Academic researchers seeking to strengthen their knowledge and practical application of Reflexive Thematic Analysis.
  • Experienced qualitative researchers looking to deepen their theoretical understanding or refine their analytical practice.

Outcomes

What will you learn?

By the end of the masterclass, you'll have developed a stronger understanding of:

Content

What does the masterclass cover?

Day 1: Understanding and practising Reflexive Thematic Analysis

The first day introduces the foundations of qualitative research before moving into an extended, practical exploration of Reflexive Thematic Analysis.

Understanding qualitative research and meaning-making

We'll begin by exploring the core principles of qualitative research, with particular attention to meaning, interpretation and the different layers of meaning that researchers can explore within their data.

Introducing Reflexive Thematic Analysis

You'll be introduced to the key concepts underpinning Reflexive Thematic Analysis, including the active role of the researcher in developing interpretations and constructing themes.

Working through the analytical process

The main focus of Day 1 is a step-by-step exploration of the analytical process, combining expert demonstrations with practical activities using a sample dataset. You'll work through:

  • Familiarising yourself with the data.
  • Generating initial codes.
  • Constructing candidate themes.
  • Reviewing and refining themes.
  • Developing and naming themes.
  • Moving towards an analytical account of the data.

Throughout the day, Claire will demonstrate the analytical process using examples from her own research, showing how interpretations and themes develop through sustained engagement with data.

You'll then have opportunities to apply what you've learned through guided activities, discussion and reflection.

Day 2: Deepening your theoretical and analytical understanding

Day 2 builds on the practical work undertaken on Day 1, exploring the theoretical foundations and methodological considerations that inform qualitative analysis.

Theoretical perspectives and knowledge generation

Explore the relationship between ontology, epistemology and qualitative research practice, including essentialist, contextualist and constructionist perspectives. You'll consider how different theoretical assumptions influence the questions researchers ask, how they engage with data and the interpretations they develop.

Understanding the thematic analysis family

Not all approaches to thematic analysis are the same. We'll examine the distinctions between Reflexive Thematic Analysis, codebook thematic analysis and coding reliability approaches, including their different assumptions about coding, themes and the researcher's role.

Researcher subjectivity and reflexivity

Explore why researcher subjectivity is understood as a resource within Reflexive Thematic Analysis, rather than simply a source of bias. We'll consider the role of reflexivity throughout the research process and how researchers can engage critically with their own assumptions, perspectives and analytical decisions.

Thematic analysis and other qualitative approaches

We'll examine how thematic analysis relates to other qualitative approaches, including discourse analysis, grounded theory and Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis (IPA).

Working with different forms of qualitative data

Finally, we'll explore how thematic analysis can be applied beyond traditional interview and focus group transcripts, including visual and multimodal data such as images and webpages.

Approach

How will you learn?

This masterclass is designed to help you understand not only what Reflexive Thematic Analysis involves, but also how to undertake it.

Rather than relying solely on presentations, Claire combines theoretical explanations with worked examples, practical exercises and interactive discussions.

A distinctive feature of the training is the use of real examples from Claire's own research. You'll see how she approached the analytical process, developed interpretations and made methodological decisions, rather than simply being shown the finished themes.

The training builds progressively across the two days, allowing you to connect theoretical concepts with practical application, ask questions and learn alongside researchers from different disciplines and backgrounds.
