Who it's for

Who is The Research Circle for?

The Research Circle is designed for researchers who want to strengthen their skills, make confident methodological decisions and maintain momentum throughout their research.

It may be particularly valuable if you:

  • Are undertaking a PhD or other higher degree by research.
  • Are an early career researcher developing your qualitative research expertise.
  • Are an experienced academic or researcher looking to deepen your methodological expertise, explore new approaches or benefit from specialist qualitative research support.
  • Are working independently or without access to specialist qualitative research support.
  • Find yourself second-guessing methodological or analytical decisions.
  • Want opportunities to discuss your research challenges with an experienced qualitative researcher.
  • Are looking for structure, accountability and connection as your research progresses.

Whether you're developing your research design, collecting data, working through analysis or writing up your findings, The Research Circle provides support relevant to your stage of research.

Outcomes

What will you gain?

Greater clarity in your research decisions

Work through methodological and analytical questions with expert guidance, helping you make decisions that are appropriate for your research questions, theoretical positioning and methodological approach.

Confidence in the quality of your work

Develop your ability to critically evaluate your research decisions, identify potential gaps and articulate the rationale underpinning your approach.

Sustained research momentum

Build realistic goals, navigate roadblocks and maintain progress through regular opportunities for reflection, planning and accountability.

Support when you need it

Rather than waiting until a problem becomes a major obstacle, bring your questions and challenges to a supportive space where you can explore possible ways forward.

Connection with other researchers

Share experiences, exchange ideas and learn alongside researchers who understand the complexities and uncertainties of qualitative research.

What's included

What's included in your membership?

All Qualitative Research Institute training

Access our complete range of individual training programs throughout your membership, including:

  • Essentials of Qualitative Research — build a strong foundation in qualitative research principles and practices.
  • Qualitative Research Design Masterclass — strengthen the methodological coherence of your research.
  • Reflexive Thematic Analysis Masterclass — develop your skills in Braun and Clarke's approach to thematic analysis.
  • Using AI in Qualitative Research Masterclass — explore the critical, ethical and methodologically defensible use of AI in qualitative research.

Participate in live training where available or access training resources on demand, revisiting relevant material as your research progresses.

Individual research coaching

Monthly research Q&A

Mindset & Momentum Mapping

Join a monthly session focused on the practical, psychological and strategic dimensions of research.

Explore strategies for navigating uncertainty, maintaining focus and building confidence, while reflecting on your progress, identifying priorities and planning your next steps.

Research resources and support

Research community and connection

How it works

How does The Research Circle work?

The Research Circle combines structured opportunities for learning and reflection with flexible support that fits around your research commitments.

Each month, you'll have access to a live research Q&A and a Mindset & Momentum Mapping session. Individual research coaching provides dedicated time to focus on your own project, while the members' hub and community offer ongoing access to resources and peer connection.

Live group sessions are recorded, so you can catch up when you cannot attend.

Your membership runs for either six or twelve months, giving you the flexibility to choose the level of ongoing support that suits your research.

Your facilitator

Meet your facilitator

Dr Claire Moran

Dr Claire Moran is the founder of The Qualitative Research Institute, an experienced qualitative researcher and educator who specialises in making complex methodological concepts accessible, practical and applicable to real research projects.

She holds a PhD in Health Psychology from the University of Queensland, where she received the Dean's Award for Outstanding Research Higher Degree Theses.

Having experienced firsthand the uncertainties and challenges of undertaking research, Claire is passionate about helping researchers develop the skills, confidence and support networks they need to produce high-quality work without navigating every challenge alone.
