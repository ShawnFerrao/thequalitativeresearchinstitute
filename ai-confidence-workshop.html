Best suited for

Qualitative researchers who are using or considering using AI and want practical guidance for making decisions about its role in their research.

Outcomes

What you'll gain

  • A practical framework for deciding whether and how to use AI for different research tasks
  • Greater clarity about where AI can appropriately support your work — and where you may want to retain researcher judgement
  • A way to evaluate AI use in relation to your methodology and research approach
  • Practical experience working through realistic AI decisions
  • Opportunities to apply the process to questions arising in your own research
  • A process for checking, explaining and documenting the AI decisions you make
Content

What we'll explore

We'll work with practical questions such as:

  • Can I use AI to support coding or analysis?
  • What about summarising transcripts or research material?
  • Can AI support literature work, writing or editing?
  • When is AI assisting my work — and when is it beginning to shape the research?
  • How does my methodology affect what is appropriate?
  • If I decide to use AI, how do I work with it deliberately and critically?
  • What should I check rather than simply accept from AI?
  • What do I need to document or disclose?

Using realistic qualitative research scenarios, we'll explore these questions through the AI Defensibility Framework and then apply the process to AI uses and decisions arising in your own research.

The workshop draws on published research, current guidance and methodological scholarship, while remaining firmly focused on the practical decisions researchers are facing now.
